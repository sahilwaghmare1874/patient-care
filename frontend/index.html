TRANSPARENT SYSTEM WORKFLOW

How The Healthcare System Works

A seamless pipeline connecting patient registration to doctor consultation, diagnostics, funding, and final benefit reports.

1. Registration & Unique Patient ID

Patients register with verified Indian PAN card. Existing PAN numbers retrieve historical records; new patients receive a permanent Patient ID (PAT-2026-XXXXXX) and HAC pass.

2. Clinical Doctor & Diagnostics

Empanelled doctors access longitudinal medical history, record diagnoses, recommend treatments, order accredited pathology investigations, and issue electronic prescriptions.

3. Government Fund & Insurance

Eligible treatments receive funding from Ayushman Bharat (PM-JAY) and state programs. If government funding has a deficit, automated secondary insurance claims step in.

4. Pharmacy Dispensation

Authorized chemists verify electronic prescriptions, dispense medications at concessional GST rates, and issue digital invoices with automatic reimbursement tracking.

5. Configurable Fund Distribution

Approved funds are distributed precisely via dynamic business rules: Doctor (10%), Pathology (10%), Pharmacy (5%), Government Admin (5%), and Patient Benefit Savings (70%).

6. Final Centralized Report

Patients, doctors, and authorities access an itemized, downloadable PDF/CSV centralized report summarizing every medical intervention, provider payout, and financial balance.

Phase 6 — Government Health Schemes

Central & Maharashtra Healthcare Programs

Discover official, verified financial assistance for major surgeries, expensive medical treatments, and hospitalization under Ayushman Bharat and Maharashtra state health initiatives.

Maharashtra State Initiative

Mahatma Jyotirao Phule Jan Arogya Yojana (MJPJAY)

₹5,00,000 / Family

Comprehensive cashless hospitalization and surgical treatment cover for identified secondary and tertiary illnesses across network empanelled hospitals in Maharashtra.

Eligibility: Holders of Yellow, Orange, or Antyodaya Anna Yojana (AAY) Ration Cards, Annapurna Card, or white ration card holder farmers from identified distressed districts.
Required Documents: Valid Ration Card, Aadhaar Card / Voter ID, and doctor's surgical recommendation.
Empanelled Facilities: Government Medical Colleges, District Hospitals, and certified private empanelled hospitals across Maharashtra.
Official Portal (jeevandayee.gov.in) Register for Assistance
Central Government Initiative

Ayushman Bharat Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana (PM-JAY)

₹5,00,000 / Year

The world's largest health assurance scheme providing fully cashless inpatient secondary and tertiary care for bottom 40% vulnerable families nationwide.

Eligibility: Deprivation and occupational criteria based on Socio-Economic Caste Census (SECC 2011) rural/urban database.
Required Documents: Ayushman Bharat Golden Card, Aadhaar Card, Mobile Number verification.
Coverage: 1,949+ medical procedures including oncology, cardiology, neurosurgery, and orthopedics nationwide.
Official Portal (pmjay.gov.in) Government Portal
National Health Fund
Rashtriya Arogya Nidhi (RAN)
Up to ₹15 Lakhs

Financial assistance for patients living below poverty line suffering from major life-threatening diseases receiving treatment at designated Super Speciality Government Hospitals / AIIMS.

Eligibility: Families below poverty line (BPL). Financial aid released directly to hospital Medical Superintendent.
Discretionary Grant
Health Minister's Discretionary Grant (HMDG)
Up to ₹1,25,000

Emergency financial assistance to poor patients with annual family income up to ₹1,25,000 for hospitalization in government hospitals when no other scheme applies.

Eligibility: Verified annual family income < ₹1.25 Lakh. Direct payment to treating government facility.
